Bhaskar

Energy from fluid force.

Exploring a new approach to generation by converting fluid–structure interactions into useful mechanical motion.

Explore the technology Collaborate with us

The question

Can we unlock useful mechanical energy from the forces already present in a fluid?

Bhaskar investigates the possibility of creating a controlled force imbalance through differences in projected area, producing motion that could ultimately be coupled to an electrical generator.

Our approach

A different way to think about energy.

01

Fluid interaction

A solid body interacts with a surrounding fluid under controlled conditions.

02

Projected-area difference

Geometry is used to create a difference in the effective area exposed to the fluid.

03

Force imbalance

The resulting difference in force can generate a net mechanical tendency for motion.

04

Mechanical output

The motion can be investigated as a potential input to an energy-conversion system.

Research status The concept is currently being developed through quantitative analysis, modelling and experimental validation. Claims about net energy output remain subject to rigorous thermodynamic and mechanical testing.

Open science mindset

We want the physics to prove the idea.

Bhaskar is being developed with an emphasis on measurable performance, transparent assumptions and independent validation. The goal is not to make extraordinary claims — it is to test an extraordinary hypothesis rigorously.
